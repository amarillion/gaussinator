How to play

Goal: turn the matrix into reduced row echelon form (RREF):

  • each leading (first non-zero) entry of a row is 1,
  • leading entries form a staircase from top-left to bottom-right,
  • every column with a leading 1 has zeros everywhere else,
  • all-zero rows sit at the bottom.

Legal moves (elementary row operations):

  • Swap two rows,
  • Multiply a row by a non-zero number k: adjust the numerator and denominator on the left factor dial; the selected row updates immediately,
  • Add one row to another: lift a row with →, pick the multiplier on the rail (the lifted copy updates live, always from the original row), align it with the target row (↑/↓), then press Enter.

Keyboard:

  • ↑ ↓ or K J — select row
  • Shift+↑/↓ — move the selected row
  • PgUp PgDn — change the numerator on the active factor dial
  • Shift+PgUp/PgDn — change the active dial's denominator
  • − — flip the active factor's sign
  • → — lift the selected row to the right; the right drawer opens and its dial sets the lifted copy's factor; ↑/↓ align it with a target row and Enter adds it (Esc or ← cancels)
  • U undo · H hint · R restart · Esc cancel / reset k

Touch: tap a row to select it, swipe up/down to swap rows, swipe right to shift (lift) a row, then swipe left on the original row to cancel or on another row to apply. The left drawer is open by default: drag or flick its numerator (−20…−1 or 1…20) and denominator (1…20) dials and the selected row scales immediately. Lifting a row closes the left drawer and opens the right one; its dials set the lifted copy's factor, which applies to the preview immediately. Needles mark the selected values, the scales snap when released, and tapping a visible number jumps to it. While a row is lifted, tap the target row to apply the lifted copy. You can also swipe the lifted rail: up/down to align it, left to apply, right to return it.
