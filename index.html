Gaussinator

2×3
Tap to select · swipe ↑/↓ swap · swipe → shift · swipe ← apply/cancel · keys: ↑↓ select · PgUp/PgDn numerator · Shift+PgUp/PgDn denominator · − negate · M scale · Enter apply · Esc cancel · U undo · H hint

How to play

Goal: turn the matrix into reduced row echelon form (RREF):

  • each leading (first non-zero) entry of a row is 1,
  • leading entries form a staircase from top-left to bottom-right,
  • every column with a leading 1 has zeros everywhere else,
  • all-zero rows sit at the bottom.

Legal moves (elementary row operations):

  • Swap two rows,
  • Multiply a row by a non-zero number k: adjust the numerator and denominator on the two scrolling dials, press M, then Enter to apply (Esc cancels),
  • Add one row to another: lift a row with →, pick the multiplier on the rail (the lifted copy updates live, always from the original row), align it with the target row (↑/↓), then press Enter.

Keyboard:

  • ↑ ↓ or K J — select row
  • Shift+↑/↓ — move the selected row
  • PgUp PgDn — change the numerator on the left dial
  • Shift+PgUp/PgDn — change the denominator on the right dial
  • − — flip the numerator's sign
  • M — start in-place scaling of the selected row; Enter applies, Esc cancels
  • → — lift the selected row to the right; the lifted copy is always the original row × the selected factor; ↑/↓ align it with a target row and Enter adds it (Esc or ← cancels)
  • U undo · H hint · R restart · Esc cancel / reset k

Touch: tap a row to select it, swipe up/down to swap rows, swipe right to shift (lift) a row, then swipe left on the original row to cancel or on another row to apply. Drag or flick the left dial to choose the numerator (−20…20) and the right dial to choose the denominator (1…20); the needle marks the selected value and the scale snaps to it when released. Tap a visible number to jump straight to it. The toolbar ×k button starts/applies in-place scaling. While a row is lifted, tap the target row to apply the lifted copy.
